Why this study is needed

We want to understand the real challenges and the critical success factors for a successful project kickoff.

Project kickoff still depends heavily on individual experience. Teams may use different templates, ask different questions or discover important gaps only after the work has started. A preliminary review of Accenture Method One did not identify one structured Labs-specific approach that brings the key kickoff elements together.

Main purpose

Understand the most common kickoff challenges and identify the critical success factors for a successful project start. The findings will be used to build a practical Labs Project Kickoff Framework.

Longer-term goal

Use the member-checked Framework as the foundation for an AI-enabled Project Kickoff Architect that can read approved project information, identify gaps and tailor kickoff support to the project.

Who is invited to participate in this study?

Experienced European Labs professionals with direct experience in project delivery, project starts or project kickoffs.

What will you do?

Complete one semi-structured questionnaire. Most questions are quick selections, with a small number of open questions for concrete examples and practical experience. Expected time: about 20–30 minutes, depending on how much detail you choose to provide in the open questions.

How will the data be analysed?

Structured answers will be summarised using descriptive statistics. Open-text answers will be coded and analysed thematically in NVivo software (research software for organising, coding and analysing qualitative data). The open-text data will be organised into themes based on recurring patterns and mechanisms, and the findings will then be translated into the Project Kickoff Framework.

What happens next?

The draft Framework will be member-checked by a small set of experienced Project Managers / Delivery Leads for credibility, clarity and usability, and then tested on a small number of real Labs projects.

Ethics and data handling. The study follows the principles of voluntary participation, data minimisation, confidentiality, transparency and human oversight. Responses should be stored and processed only in approved Accenture systems and used for this initiative, Framework member checking and pilot activities.

Anonymity

The research dataset is designed to be anonymous. The questionnaire does not ask for your name, email address, client name or project name. Please do not include names or identifying details in open-text answers.

Please do not include in your answers

  • client or project names;
  • names of colleagues;
  • personal data;
  • confidential or commercially sensitive information.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question and stop before submitting. Because no identifying field is collected, it may not be possible to identify and remove one individual response after submission.

Human oversight

AI-supported outputs are treated as support only. Findings, themes, Framework decisions and future agent recommendations remain subject to human review and judgement.

What will the questionnaire cover?

1. Your Accenture project experience 2. Current kickoff experience 3. Critical success factors 4. Project conditions that change kickoff needs 5. Critical project information & onboarding 6. AI-supported project kickoff 7. Final reflections
